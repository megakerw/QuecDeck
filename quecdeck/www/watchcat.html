Configuration summary
  • Reboots the modem after failed rounds in a row (in as little as , since failed pings can return immediately).
  • If the link stays down, the minimum wait after each reboot attempt doubles, up to two hours. Ping failures during that wait do not trigger another reboot.
  • Reboot attempts are recorded on the Logs page.
  • the configured retry window is only ~ seconds, so a brief network blip can trigger an unnecessary reboot. Raise the interval to at least seconds, or allow more failed rounds, to keep a full-minute margin.
